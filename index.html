🔴 Interplanetary Survival Guide — Martian Map

Mission: Gale Crater → Mount Sharp

Science goal: examine the layered terrain of the Mount Sharp region, where Curiosity studied boxwork formations (the example used in NASA's challenge brief).
  1. Start at Bradbury Landing (Curiosity's 2012 landing site, marked on the map).
  2. Switch on layers (top right): elevation, thermal infrared, geologic units.
  3. Press Plan Route, then click waypoints. Total distance and an estimated walking time update as you go.
Data types in view: optical (Viking), topographic (MOLA), thermal infrared (THEMIS), geologic (USGS). No radiation or hazard layer yet.
